Project / 01
AI Research · Data Pipeline

Real-Estate Agent Research Engine

Client
Kap Rate (broker intelligence)
Built on

Pre-call brief in < 90 s

A sales team needed deep, structured intelligence on individual real-estate agents — production volume, brokerage, license status, social posture, fit score — before picking up the phone. Manual research was taking 30–45 minutes per agent. I replaced it with a single webhook call.

The pipeline takes only a name and a phone number, then runs four phases: identity discovery, parallel platform scraping, gap-filling fallback, and a single LLM synthesis pass. The model layer is deliberately abstracted through OpenRouter so the brief can be regenerated on any frontier model without touching the workflow.

§ Business Case / project 01
The problem

Sales reps were burning 30–45 minutes per agent stitching together intel from 7 different sources before every cold call.

What it replaces

A junior research analyst or BDR doing manual lookups across MLS profiles, license boards, and social platforms.

The buyer

Real-estate intelligence platforms, brokerage CRMs, lead-gen tools — anyone running outbound on agent personas.

Pricing model

Metered API: priced per research call. The engine bills with usage; no seat or subscription gate.

Project / 02
Voice AI · CRM Orchestration

White-label AI Caller & Booker

Engagement
US client · freelance · Oct 2025
Built on
GoHighLevel · n8n · Retell AI
Outputs
Booked meeting · CRM tag · email

A productised service offer. The client books a discovery call, hands over their GoHighLevel sub-account, n8n instance, and Retell credentials, and I ship them a deployed AI caller in days — branded to their business, trained on their offer, integrated end-to-end with their CRM.

The flow: a paid ad routes to a GHL form. Form submission fires a GHL workflow that tags the lead, waits, then webhooks into n8n. n8n calls a Retell agent which executes four jobs in one conversation — introduce the business, qualify the lead, book the meeting, end gracefully. Post-call, n8n updates the GHL contact, fires confirmation email, and tags by outcome (Qualified / Not Qualified / Voicemail / No Answer).

§ Business Case / project 02

The buyer

Marketing agencies (white-labelling it to their service-business clients) and direct service operators running paid traffic.

Pricing model

Setup fee plus monthly retainer per client deployment, or an agency licence for unlimited sub-accounts.

Project 02 productised; this is project 02 productified. I took the same voice-AI core and built it into a vertical SaaS for HVAC contractors — a 24/7 phone receptionist that qualifies callers, books service windows against the technician's Google Calendar, and never lets an inbound lead hit a busy signal.

The customer-facing layer is a Next.js dashboard where contractors configure their agent, watch live call transcripts, review booked jobs, and manage their team's availability. The agent core stays the same Retell + n8n stack — but wrapped in a tenant-aware control plane so dozens of contractors can run off one infrastructure spine.

§ Business Case / project 03
The problem

HVAC contractors miss 30–60% of inbound calls after-hours and during peak hours. Every missed call is a job booked with the next contractor on Google.

What it replaces

An after-hours answering service or a part-time receptionist — at a fraction of the cost, with zero handoff lag.

The buyer

HVAC and home-service contractors directly (SaaS), and home-services agencies who white-label the stack to their roster.

Pricing model

Monthly subscription per contractor seat with a usage-based call ceiling, plus an agency licence for resellers.
